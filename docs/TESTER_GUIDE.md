# AfterMe beta: guide for testers

*Thank you. You are helping to check that an app that deals with something serious behaves exactly as it says. This guide is short; please read all of it before you start.*

## 1. What AfterMe is, and what it is not

AfterMe lets you choose people (your **trusted contacts**) and information (your **instructions**). You check in on a schedule you set. If you don't, AfterMe first reminds **you**. Only if you still don't answer, and only after the plan you set, does it tell your contacts and, later, share the information you chose for each of them. Pressing **I'M SAFE** stops it and tells everyone it was told that you are fine.

- It is **not an emergency service.** If anyone is in danger, call your local emergency number.
- It cannot know *why* a check-in was missed. It only follows your plan.
- It is **not a will** or a legal document.
- It is a **test version.** Things will go wrong. That is why you are here.

## 2. What we ask of you

For **14 days**: keep the app installed, check in as you normally would, and try the small scenarios in section 5 on the days suggested. About 5 minutes a day, and one longer session in each of the two weeks. Tell us what confused you, what broke and what felt wrong, however small.

## 3. Before you start

1. **You must be 18 or over.**
2. **Only add contacts who have agreed.** Tell each person first: "I'm testing an app. It may send you a test email from AfterMe, and possibly a message saying I have not checked in. It is a test and I'm fine." A good, safe choice is a second email address of your own.
3. **Use made-up information in your instructions** (for example "The spare key is under the green pot" about a house that does not exist). The content is encrypted and never appears in email or notifications, but this is a test version and we do not want real sensitive details in it.
4. **Your safety does not depend on this app.** Do not stop doing anything you would normally do to stay in touch with people.
5. Have your phone's **screen lock** on if you can. It lets the app lock protect what is on screen.

## 4. Setting up (15 minutes)

1. Open the invitation link on your phone and install AfterMe from Google Play.
2. Create your account with your email and a password you do not use elsewhere. Enter the 6-digit code that is emailed to you.
3. Follow the steps: your name, how often to check in and by when, your first contact, a short instruction, and the plan (who is told, and when).
4. Allow notifications when asked (you can change your mind later).
5. Ask your contact to **accept the invitation** in the email they receive. They do not need to install anything.
6. Press **I'M OK** once to check in.

Write down the time of your first check-in and the contact's address you used (just for you).

## 5. Scenarios

Do these when it suits you in the 14 days. Each is a normal use, not a trick.

| # | When | What to try | What you should see |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1 | Finish setup and check in once | Home says you are checked in and shows the next time |
| 2 | Every day | Check in with one tap | Immediate confirmation; Home updates |
| 3 | Days 2–3 | Put the phone in airplane mode, tap **I'M OK**, then turn the network on | It says it will keep trying, then confirms once online. Check **Settings → Check-in history** |
| 4 | Day 3 | Close the app completely and open it from the notification when a reminder arrives | The reminder is generic (no names, no information) and opens the app |
| 5 | Days 4–5 | **Rehearsal:** with your contact's agreement, deliberately miss one check-in. *First send us the "I am testing a missed check-in today" note (section 6) so we know it is on purpose.* Wait for the reminder, then for the alert time you set | Reminders first; later your contact gets a clear message that you have not checked in; Home explains what has happened and offers **I'M SAFE** |
| 6 | When #5 has alerted your contact | Press **I'M SAFE** (the app asks for your password) | Your contact gets an all-clear; Activity lists what happened |
| 7 | Week 2 | **Lost phone rehearsal:** on a laptop or another phone, open the web check-in page (the app shows its address under **Settings → Help and about → If you lose your phone**; it ends in `/a/login`) and check in or press I'M SAFE there | It works with your email and password only; you get an email saying someone signed in on the web |
| 8 | Week 2, **only if we have switched on Premium for you** (the free version allows one contact and a short plan) | Add an instruction for your contact and give it a stage a day out. Release is never earlier than 24 hours after a missed check-in, so this takes a day: miss a check-in on a day we agreed, **do not** press I'M SAFE until you have opened the link, then press it | The contact's email has a link, not the information. The page asks for a 6-digit code sent to the contact, then shows the information. After I'M SAFE the page no longer shows it |
| 9 | Week 2 | Turn on **App lock** (Settings → Security); leave the app in the background for a few minutes; come back | It asks you to unlock. Check what happens if you have no fingerprint set up |
| 10 | Week 2 | **Settings → Privacy and data → Export my data** | You receive a file you can save. It contains your information, so delete it afterwards |
| 11 | Last day | If you wish: delete your account (**Settings → Privacy and data → Delete account**) | You are told what will happen to contacts; they are told the alert (if any) is withdrawn; you are signed out |

If a step does not do what the table says, **that is exactly what we need to hear about.**

## 6. How to tell us

Use the feedback form we sent you (one entry per problem). Please include:

- **What you did, what you expected, what happened** (a sentence each).
- The **time** it happened.
- The **diagnostic text**: *Settings → Help and about → About AfterMe → Copy diagnostic text*, then paste it. It contains only the app version, the build number, the environment and the server address, never your password or information. Please add your phone make, model and Android version yourself.
- A **screenshot** only if it shows nothing private. (Some screens are deliberately blocked from screenshots; that is intended.)
- For a rehearsal: use the form entry **"I am testing a missed check-in today"** *before* you start.

**Urgent** (a message went to a contact when it should not have; you could not check in; you saw someone else's information): message us **immediately** on the number or address on the invitation, then fill in the form.

## 7. Your information

- We keep your account, your contacts' names and addresses, your instructions (encrypted) and your check-in history so that the app can work. The privacy policy at the link in the app describes this in full.
- Your contacts may receive emails from AfterMe: invitations, alerts, and a link to what you chose to share. They can decline or opt out of any of it at any time from the email.
- We never sell data and there is no advertising.
- **Leaving:** at any time, use *Settings → Delete account*, or the web page in the privacy policy. Your contacts' details are removed from our systems after a short delay so that anyone who was told something can be told it was withdrawn.
- At the end of the beta we will tell you, before it happens, whether accounts are kept or reset.

## 8. Things worth knowing

- **Reminders come first and only to you.** Nobody else hears anything during your grace period.
- **Some actions ask for your password again** (changing the plan, reading an instruction, I'M SAFE). That is on purpose.
- The app may say **"We had an interruption"** if we paused sending while fixing something. Nothing is sent to contacts during that time.
- If the app says an **update is required**, update from Google Play; that message means the old version could not be trusted to work.

Thank you again.
