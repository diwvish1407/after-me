## 2. Who may use AfterMe

You must be **18 or older** and able to enter into a contract. You must provide accurate information and keep your password secret. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

## 3. What AfterMe does

AfterMe lets you set a check-in schedule, choose trusted contacts, prepare instructions, and decide who is told what and when if you do not check in. We carry out **the plan you set**. We do not decide for you, and we cannot know why a check-in was missed.

## 4. What we promise, and what we do not

- **AfterMe is not an emergency service.** We do not contact police, ambulance or any authority. If you or anyone is in danger, call your local emergency number.
- **Best effort.** We work hard to follow your plan at the times you chose, with safeguards that delay or pause actions when our own systems are unwell. We **do not guarantee** that every reminder, alert or message is delivered, or delivered on time: email, push and phone networks are outside our control. Do not rely on AfterMe as your only way for someone to reach you or to learn that you are unreachable.
- **Not a will or a legal instrument.** Instructions in AfterMe do not have legal effect, do not replace a will, power of attorney, medical directive or any legal document, and do not transfer any property or right.
- **Not advice.** Nothing in AfterMe is legal, medical, financial or safety advice.
- **Information shared with contacts is shared as you configured it.** Once a contact has read an instruction, we cannot take it back.
- We may change, suspend or stop features, and will tell you in advance of changes that affect your plan.

## 5. Your responsibilities

- Keep your check-in schedule, contacts, plan and instructions accurate and up to date.
- Only add people whom you may lawfully contact and who would expect to hear from you. You are responsible for having the right to give us their details.
- Make sure what you write in instructions is lawful and does not infringe anyone's rights. Do not store information in AfterMe that you are not allowed to share.
- Keep your phone and email secure. Enable your phone's screen lock. Tell us promptly if you think your account was accessed by someone else.
- Tell your contacts that AfterMe may email them, and what it means.

## 6. Acceptable use

You will not use AfterMe to harass, threaten, deceive or spam anyone; to impersonate someone; to send messages to people who have not agreed or who have opted out; to attack, probe or overload the service; to bypass limits or security; or to break the law. We may suspend or end accounts that do, and we may refuse to send a message if we believe it would cause harm.

## 7. Contacts

People you add as trusted contacts may accept, decline or opt out at any time from any AfterMe email. If someone chooses "never contact me about AfterMe again", AfterMe will not message them for any user; if they only stop emails from you, that applies to you. Their choice overrides your plan.
